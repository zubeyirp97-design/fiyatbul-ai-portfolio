PriceFinder AI

An AI-Powered Second-Hand Pricing and Listing Assistant

PriceFinder AI helps users estimate second-hand item prices, generate listing descriptions, and prepare negotiation responses with AI.

- AI-powered price estimation
- Suggested listing prices
- Ready-to-use marketplace descriptions
- AI-assisted negotiation responses

Market Focus: Designed specifically for the Turkish second-hand market, with price estimates in Turkish Lira (TRY).
